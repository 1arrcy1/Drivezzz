# Drivezzz

A ride app in the style of Bolt and Uber, as an open market: drivers set their own prices, riders pay in crypto.
iOS app `eu.drivezzz.app`, website and admin panel at https://drivezzz.s3rver.nl (server: `your-server`, port 8080).

## The code
Public on [GitHub](https://github.com/1arrcy1/Drivezzz) under the [Drivezzz Source Licence](LICENSE): read it, check it and build your own on it, with your own smart contract if you like, but don't copy Drivezzz or use its name. So it's source-available, not open source. A version of your own runs on your own servers and its own escrow program; Drivezzz's servers are for Drivezzz's app, under its [Terms](https://drivezzz.s3rver.nl/terms) and [EULA](https://drivezzz.s3rver.nl/eula).

- `drivezzz/`: the iPhone app in SwiftUI (`drivezzz.xcodeproj`, iOS 17+).
- `android/`: the same app in Kotlin and Compose.
- `server/`: the server in Node.js with no packages, the website (`public/`) and the admin panel (`admin/`). `npm test` runs its tests.
- `contract/`: the escrow program on Solana, in Rust, with tests that run on devnet.
- `docs/`: the escrow, capacity, Android and the Dutch driver checks.

Found a security problem? [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) says how to tell us.

## For riders
- **Home**: Now or "Plan a ride" first, then "Where to?": type right in the panel (results show under it, the pickup can be changed there too) or tap the map. Swipe down for the map of drivers within your filter (10 km, up to 40). A drop-off off the road moves to where the road ends: the map shows where you tapped, dashed to the flag.
- **Quote**: pickup and drop-off move to the closest spot a car can stop. Every driver's price includes their way to you (voorrijkosten). The cheapest is picked unless you choose one.
- **Holding the map** moves the pickup: that's where the driver waits, in orange on every map. Holding a driver's pin, or tapping the vehicle's picture in the list, opens their page.
- **Tapping a driver**, on the map or in the list, picks them: their pin turns dark and the list marks them. Before there's a destination, "Book with …" asks where to and books with them; with a route, Book books them. Tapping the picked driver again opens their page. A tap on or near a driver's pin never moves the drop-off.
- **Saved routes**: the bookmark on a route keeps its pickup and drop-off under a name, in Rides under Booked. One tap fills in Home. They stay on the phone.
- **The map shows the 5 cheapest drivers** around you, each as a circle with their vehicle, with a short line up to a bubble with their price and their stars or New. The list has up to 50 to choose from: its title or the filter sorts it by cheapest, best rated, fastest or on time. A tap on a circle zooms in on it.
- **Choose**: people, pet, car/motor/scooter, coin, now or planned (up to 7 days).
- **Your price**: name a price; drivers see it on their map and can claim it. You can raise it, and the app asks you to confirm what you add and the new total.
- **Ordering**: one order at a time. "Book" (or "Book now" on a driver's page) says "Waiting for driver to accept" with a 5-minute countdown; you can cancel for free, but not order another ride or book another driver until it's over. Pickup and destination show on the map.
- **Planning**: "Plan" never gives the ride to anyone by itself: it waits as "Planned, no driver yet" until a driver who plans claims it. A driver you chose gets a push and has it to themselves for 30 minutes (or half the time that's left), then any driver can claim it. If a driver passes on it before you've paid, it goes back to waiting.
- **Paying**: ordering is free and needs no connected wallet. Once a driver accepts or claims it you get a notification and "Waiting for payment", and the page opens by itself: 5 minutes (planned: until 30 minutes before) to pay in USDC or SOL from your own wallet: pick the wallet app, approve there, come back. You can still cancel. Not paid in time: cancelled, nothing moved. Nothing is locked in the contract before you've paid.
- **How you pay**: Book, Plan and naming your price all ask at the end: Cash, SOL or USDC on Solana, as far as that driver takes it. The chip above the list only filters drivers. Cash says plainly that you have less protection and that it's finalised with the driver.
- **Cash**: pick Cash instead of a coin and there's no paying step and no wallet: the driver goes straight on, you pay them in the car. Check-in and check-out are the same, and once the driver has checked out the ride says "Paid in cash".
- **The driver coming**: the map shows the car and about how long it needs. The driver's time to reach you is what the way takes plus 30%, plus the 5 minutes for paying (a 50-minute way: 70 minutes).
- **Cancelling**: free until you've paid. Once paid, 50/50 from that moment while the driver is on time. Is the driver late, the button turns green: a full refund until you've checked in, also if the driver has arrived by then, with advice to look at the map first. A planned ride is a full refund until 3 hours before, then 50/50; its driver's time ends 10 minutes after the planned time.
- **Check-in** only counts once you're inside the car; being at the pickup isn't enough. The app asks you to say so, within 100 m of the car.
- **Reviews**: when you've arrived, a review page opens by itself: stars and some text if you like, or "Not now". It shows on the driver's profile.
- **A driver's profile** shows how often they complete a ride they took and how often they're on time, in percent, with their rating and reviews. A rider cancelling doesn't count against them.
- **iPhone Duo**: screens are laid out by the room they have. Open, the panel stands beside the map, clear of the fold, and pages stop at a readable width; closed, the short screen works with the bars at the side. It fills the Duo's screens once built with Xcode 27.1 (`APP_STORE.md`, section 1c).
- **Messages**: hold a chat, or tap the top of one, to open a driver's profile. The Chat tab, between Rides and Profile (for drivers: top right on Drives, whose tab shows the unread count). You can message a driver from their page or a ride, end-to-end encrypted: the server only stores what it can't read, and checks who may send. Up to 3 messages in a row; a driver can only reply after 3, and then each side can send 3 more at a time. Swipe a chat to remove it, or use the menu inside it: it's gone from your own list, the other person keeps theirs, and a new message brings it back. Once both have removed it the messages are deleted. A report adds your chat with that person, readable, to the report in the admin panel. A new iPhone reads old chats through iCloud Keychain.
- **Your wallet**: addresses only come from connecting a wallet, never typed or pasted, so they can't be wrong. Each shows the wallet app it came from. The first wallet connects freely; after that it's locked, so someone who gets into an account can't send a driver's pay elsewhere: "Change wallet" is gray, "Request wallet change" sends support a message, and once an admin allows it on the panel (Wallet changes) the button is blue for one change, then gray again. Connecting the same wallet again, or on the other coin, needs nothing. Rides already paid keep the address they were paid with. Removing an address only goes through support (admin panel, user, Remove). "Another wallet" shows a WalletConnect QR and link instead of opening whatever app iOS picks. Trust Wallet goes through WalletConnect, Phantom and Solflare through their own links, and MetaMask through its own protocol, MetaMask Connect, as its phone app doesn't share Solana over WalletConnect. MetaMask doesn't jump back by itself on that protocol: it says "Return to the app", and iOS shows a way back in the top left. The Wallet screen shows no balance: what's on hold per ride, with a link to the chain. A rider doesn't need a connected wallet: they pick one when paying.
- **Rides**: current, booked, completed. Follow the driver and see the plate.
- **Check in**: opens by itself within 100 m of the driver: check the plate, get in, check in. Can be dismissed and reopened.
- **Safety**: both phones are compared along the way; if they drift apart the rider is asked if all is well (with a call to 112).
- **Afterwards**: rate the driver; appeal within 24 hours. If your phone wasn't within 500 m of the drop-off at check-out (or unknown), you both get a heads-up, which you can ignore if you were dropped off correctly; nothing is held by that alone.

## For drivers
- **Driving**: a map with your vehicle, today's earnings, how often you're on time and your acceptance rate. Slide to go online: your position is shared with iOS's blue bar, also with the app in the background, until you slide back or close the app. Reconnects by itself. The panel also shows orders nearby, planned drives and prices in your area. Your vehicle stands still when GPS only wobbles (within the fix's accuracy, at least 15 m as on the server, or while the phone says it isn't moving) and moves for a real move; riders see your car the same way, holding still under 30 m and gliding to a real move.
- **Navigation**: turn-by-turn inside the app on Apple's routes, with voice (can be muted): to the rider, then to the drop-off. It reroutes when you leave the route and keeps the screen on. Apple Maps, Google Maps and Waze are one tap away.
- **Map**: orders closest or highest first, within the range you set (10 km, up to 40, on Driving and Map); swipe down for the map. Green is now, yellow is booked for a time. What you've booked yourself, now or planned, shows there too with a check before its price, on Driving and Map; a tap opens that ride (the one on now opens its directions). A tap goes to the map, which asks which pin was under the finger, generously. A car sees car orders, a motor or scooter its own kind; "any vehicle" orders show to everyone. With a rider on board you can't claim another drive, planned ones neither.
- **Offers**: a request shows its route on a map, the way to the pickup, the ride and the total distance, so you can judge the fare. 5 minutes to accept. Swipe it down and it waits in Current on Drives, with the same time running. Not answering puts you offline, also with drive mode on, until you go online yourself; the app says why. The offer goes to the next cheapest driver, then the market. An order opens with Apple's route to the pickup and on to the drop-off, and says the way to the pickup isn't paid on a rider's named price. Online in the background, a new offer is a notification: the server pushes it (APNs key 93NT9RV7VH, team N92R3SAXJ7, in `/etc/drivezzz-apns.p8` via `deploy/turn-on-push.sh`); without a key the app says it itself.
- **Licences**: Drivezzz is in the Netherlands only for now, and a car there goes through **Driving in the Netherlands** before it can go online (a motor or scooter doesn't). The ID's chip, a selfie matched on the phone, the chauffeurskaart and taxi permit (scanned, read on the phone, signed with App Attest and compared on the server), KvK, the RDW (a taxi, insured, APK, not exported) and the plate read from the car's back photo. What passes approves by itself; what can't be confirmed waits on the admin panel's Driver checks tab with the reason, and only a person can say no. The driver only hears that something is waiting for review, never why, and only a person asks for something again, after review. The ID's name comes from its chip, not from the camera's scan of the back. When everything's in and only a person's look is left (the documents, or a car's plate that wasn't read from its back photo), going online doesn't open the checks again: it says what is under review, usually within 24 hours, and what you can do meanwhile (drive another vehicle that's cleared, or take the back photo again). The car's page and My page say "Plate under review" too. Verified drivers get a blue check on their profile and in riders' lists. Kiwa has no register, so a good fake card or a withdrawn one still gets through. `docs/driver-checks-nl.md` has it all.
- **Going offline** waits until the drive is finished or cancelled.
- **Drives**: current, planned (an agenda by day) and completed, with each day's earnings; the chat is top right.
- **My page**: cars, motors and scooters, each with its own prices, pets and seats (a motor or scooter takes one passenger); one is active. Tapping one shows it the way riders see it (pictures, prices, rating, reviews), with Edit top right. A Dutch plate fills in the rest from the RDW and gives generated pictures; your own photos show first. Planning on or off, and how many days ahead (1 to 7).
- **A drive**: "Go to rider" shows the directions on the Driving tab's own map, not on a page of its own. After accepting you can start right away: it says "Waiting for payment" with a 5-minute countdown. A push says when it's paid. Not paid in time: cancelled, nothing for anyone. Planned drives show "Not paid yet" until then.
- **Earnings**: the panel on the Driving tab stays minimized at today's earnings; slide it up for the rest.
- **Photos**: a vehicle can't go online without photos of its front and back, and a car also of the inside. My page says what's missing.
- **Check out**: within 200 m of the drop-off, and the driver's button stays grey until then. The rider's phone far away only warns both. A driver who never came within 300 m can't be paid; no check-out within 24 hours of the expected arrival refunds the rider, unless the driver appeals.
- **Cash**: a driver takes cash unless they turn it off in Wallet, and sees it on the offer or order before saying yes. It counts in their earnings, and their wallet never shows it.
- **Wallet**: going online needs a connected wallet. USDC only reaches a wallet with a USDC account, so without one USDC stays off and the screen says how to get one (swap a little SOL to USDC once). SOL rides work regardless.
- **Cancelling** refunds the rider, also once they've checked in, with one exception: 50% for you, paid by itself after 24 hours, when you stopped at the pickup (the 10 minutes start when the car stands still), waited 10 minutes there and the rider didn't check in: cancel with "Rider not there". An appeal freezes that money for a manual review; nobody deciding within 30 days refunds the rider.
- **Riding or driving**: you switch in Profile, but not during a ride or drive: from ordering until the ride is over, or from accepting until the drive is over. A ride or drive planned for later doesn't count until it starts; when it does, the app goes to that side by itself. The server refuses the same: no going online with a ride of your own, no ordering during a drive.

## Money
**The look**: the map is the market: each driver is a circle with their vehicle, with a short line up to a bubble with their own price and stars or New (per km, then the whole ride once you've picked a destination). Zooming out the bubbles go first, past 12 km across, then the circles, past 30 km, so the map stays clear; only a driver you chose stays. Best rated counts how many reviews a driver has, so one 5-star review doesn't come first. Name your price on a ruler. A ride's progress is a road a car drives along; a payment landing gets its own moment ("Locked in escrow"); and there's a dark mode everywhere, by itself when it's dark outside or by choice in Profile.

USDC and SOL on Solana, or cash. Drivezzz holds no money: no deposits, balances or withdrawals. Each ride is paid from the rider's own wallet into its own escrow, a small program on Solana (`contract/`, plan and results in `docs/escrow.md`). The money can only go to the wallet that paid, the driver and the fee address.
- **Paying**: the server builds the payment for the wallet's address (`src/escrow.js`, no library), the wallet signs it, the server sends it on: Phantom and Solflare through their `signTransaction` links, MetaMask through its multichain API's `signTransaction` (`Core/MetaMaskConnect.swift`, mainnet only on its phone app), the others through WalletConnect's `solana_signTransaction`. A Solana Pay link and QR are there too, but on iOS that link opens whichever wallet the phone picks. The server only sends a payment into that ride's own escrow, and sees it land by reading the chain.
- **Outcomes**: the server's key picks one per escrow: pay out (price minus €0.10 to the driver; the €0.10 to `6aHmEJ6gikE7XH8NANwzBHukdAP2nMUkQR9DTRkV7YuN`, or the Solana address connected on the admin panel's Money page), 50/50 (the rider gets any odd unit) or refund. It can't send money anywhere else. A retry can't pay twice: the program allows one outcome per escrow, and the signature is stored before sending.
- **Stepping in**: on the admin panel's Money page or in a ride, an admin can hold any paid ride, release it, or refund, split or pay it out right now, with or without an appeal. It goes out straight away. Nothing gets past a hold except the refund after 30 days.
- **Timing**: paid out 24 hours after check-out, unless someone appeals. An appeal holds it until an admin decides. A driver's appeal after at least 60% of the way pays the driver after 3 days if nobody decided. After 30 days the program lets anyone refund the rider in full; the server does it on the day.
- **Fees**: the rider pays the network fees (about €0.001) and the escrow's deposit (about €0.11 for SOL, €0.23 for USDC), which comes back when it closes. The server's key needs about 0.01 SOL as a float: each settle pays back its own fee.
- Amounts are fixed in the coin at payment, at Kraken's price. Only the chain counts as proof of money.
- **Cash** (`coin` is `cash`): goes from rider to driver in the car, past `awaiting_payment` and the escrow. So nothing can be refunded, split or held: cancelling costs nobody anything, a no-show or a cancel in the car claims nothing, and there's no €0.10, as there's nothing to take it from. Its payout row has status `cash`, the whole price and nothing to send; it's only there for the driver's earnings. An appeal still reaches an admin for 24 hours, who can answer and close it.
- Old rides and `deposits`/`ledger` rows stay in the database as history. XRP, LTC and XLM show on old rides only.

## Accounts and bans
Continue opens the End User Licence Agreement, and accepting it makes a user ID (`dz-xxxxxx`) that never changes; the name can be anything. A login (email, password) is optional, in Profile. Without one there's no Log out, as there'd be nothing to log back in with: only Delete account. Every strike is a one-week ban; three is permanent. Strikes stay with the phone: they count for every account that ever used it, also after deleting one. While banned there's no logging out or deleting the account, only appealing, and "Check again". A ban covers the account and its phones, and the phone's id is in the Keychain, so reinstalling the app or making a new account doesn't end it. A permanent ban also covers its wallet addresses. The ban screen has no log out: all you can do is appeal. One appeal per 24 hours; after a decision, no more from the app.

## Safety and the App Store
Report and block from a driver's page or a ride; blocked people aren't matched either way. Names, bios, vehicle names, reviews and place names go through a word filter. Support form at `/support`. See `APP_STORE.md` for submitting.
The App Review login (`/root/drivezzz-review-login.txt`) lives in a separate demo world: simulated drivers and riders around wherever it is, a ride that plays out by itself, demo coins, and nothing that ever reaches real people or the chain. Remake it with `src/make-review-account.js`.

## Admin panel (`/admin`)
Who's online, users, ban and strike, reports, ban appeals, ride appeals (refund, 50/50, pay out), rides with what happened, the planned route on a map, and both phones' positions to play back (one minute of the ride per second), money (the escrow program, its settings, what's held, escrows with their links to the chain and any error, and Hold, Refund, Split 50/50 and Pay driver on each), banned wallets, switching network, wallet change requests (Allow, Decline, Take back), support messages, removing photos, reviews and names, a log.

You log in with an email and password. The password is stored only as a hash in the database. Set the login with `src/make-admin.js` (below); that first password only gets you to a screen that asks for a new one, and nothing else works until you've chosen it. Change it later with Change password at the top; that logs out other browsers. Forgot it? Run the same command again, as root on the server:
```
cd /opt/drivezzz/server && runuser -u drivezzz -- env DATA_DIR=/var/lib/drivezzz ADMIN_EMAIL=you@example.com ADMIN_PASSWORD=temporary /opt/drivezzz/node/bin/node --disable-warning=ExperimentalWarning src/make-admin.js
```

## Going live with real money
`main` is live on the real networks since 2026-11-02, with the old server wallets. This branch replaces that with the escrow; `docs/escrow.md` section 21 has the steps to switch. The old wallets (`/root/keys.txt`, `/root/drivezzz-wallets.json`) stay untouched: Pieter reads them himself. Nightly database backup in `/root/drivezzz-backups` (14 days, same disk).

## Push
On since 2026-11-02: Apple's key is in `/etc/drivezzz-apns.p8` and its settings in `/etc/default/drivezzz` (`server/deploy/turn-on-push.sh` installs them). The server pushes what happens; a phone with push doesn't also show the app's own alert for it. Signing out stops alerts to that phone. Apple's refusals are in the service log as `push refused`.

## Running it
- Server: Node 24, no packages. `./deploy.sh` copies it to the server and restarts it; data and keys aren't touched.
- Test server: always on at `http://your-server:8090` on the home network, with a pretend chain (no contract, no real money), its own data and a pretend driver who answers every order after 20 seconds and drives the ride. `./deploy-test.sh` updates it and never touches the live server. Point a debug build at it with `serverURLOverride`; Profile's last line says which server you're on. With no chain (`ESCROW_FAKE`, or `PRETEND_CHAIN=1` on the live server until the contract is deployed) the pay card has a test button in debug builds instead of the wallets. While the live server is on that pretend chain it also has four pretend drivers to book near Den Haag Centraal (`deploy/drivezzz-live-test-driver.service` and `deploy/drivezzz-live-test-driver@.service`). The driver checks on the live server are real: only those four, running on the server itself, pass them pretend.
- App: open `drivezzz.xcodeproj`, scheme `drivezzz`.
- Android: `android/`, the same app in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, screen for screen, with the same server: `cd android && ./gradlew :app:assembleQa` (a release build on the test server) or `:app:installDebug`. A debug build talks to the live server, as an iPhone debug build does: set `serverURLOverride` first (`adb shell am start -n eu.drivezzz.app/.MainActivity --es serverURLOverride http://your-server:8090`) before anything that makes accounts. It has the driver checks too (`features/checks/`: the ID's chip with JMRTD, text and faces read on the phone with ML Kit, SFace in plain Kotlin), signed with Android's Key Attestation instead of App Attest (`AppAttest.kt`, `verifyAndroidAttestation`). The server takes APKs signed with this Mac's debug key (`assembleLive`, `assembleQa`) or the upload key (`assembleRelease`); Google Play's own app signing key still has to be added to `ANDROID_SIGNERS` in `attest.js` (Play Console, App integrity), or what a Play install sends waits for a person. The panel's driver check says which phones sign for an account. `docs/android.md` has how to run it, what's tried and what Pieter still decides. Android 16 for now; it should look almost the same as iOS. `PLAY_STORE.md` has everything for Google Play.
- Tests on the server, as Node isn't on the Mac: copy `server/` to a temp folder and run `npm test` with `/opt/drivezzz/node/bin` on PATH. `test/reference/` has checks against the chains' own libraries and real test-network trips.
- The escrow: `contract/` is built on the server as user `dzbuild` (Rust and Solana's tools are there). `contract/test/devnet.mjs` runs every outcome and attack against the devnet program; `contract/test/e2e-server.mjs` runs the server and the program together on devnet, against a `DEV=1` instance.
