5. No tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. That includes threats, harassment, hate, discrimination, sexual or violent content, and fraud, in names, photos, reviews, messages or in person.

  • We look at reports within 24 hours, remove what breaks these rules and ban those responsible.
  • Every strike is a one-week ban, and three strikes is a permanent ban. A ban also covers your phone and, once your ID was checked for driving, you. Section 12 of the Terms has the details, and how to appeal.

6. What Drivezzz is

Drivezzz is a marketplace that brings riders and drivers together. We don't offer transport and don't employ drivers. The agreement for a ride is between the rider and the driver, and we aren't a party to it.

  • Drivers set their own prices. They're responsible for their licences, permits, insurance, taxes and a safe, road-legal vehicle.
  • We take no responsibility for licences or insurance, but we do check what we can. A blue check is no guarantee: a document can be false or withdrawn without us seeing it, and anything can change after we checked.
  • We don't screen riders.

7. On the road

  • In an emergency, call 112 or the local emergency number. The app isn't an emergency service and can't call for help.
  • If you drive, the road comes first. Directions, routes and times are a guide and can be wrong or out of date: follow the traffic rules, the signs and what you see. Set up the app before you leave, and only touch your phone when that's safe and legal.
  • If you ride, check the number plate before you get in, and check in only once you're inside. If something doesn't feel right, don't get in.

8. Wallets, crypto and cash

Drivezzz isn't a bank, a wallet or an exchange. It has no deposits, balances or withdrawals and doesn't hold your coins. Your wallet is your own: we never see its keys or recovery words, so we can't get it back for you if you lose them.

  • A payment in USDC or SOL goes from your own wallet into an escrow, a program on the Solana blockchain, for that one ride. Section 7 of the Terms says where it can go from there.
  • A payment on a blockchain can't be reversed. Check what your wallet asks you to approve.
  • Crypto can lose value. A refund returns the amount of coin you paid, whatever it's worth by then.
  • The escrow program has had no paid security audit, so a bug could lock or misroute money. Only pay what you can afford to lose.
  • Wallet apps, the Solana network and the connections to it are run by others, and can be slow or stop working.
  • Nothing in the app is financial, investment or tax advice. Taxes on what you earn or pay are your own responsibility.
  • If you pay in cash, it's between you and the driver. We never handle cash, so nothing is held, refunded or split, and you have less protection.

9. Your location and your phone

  • The app needs your precise location to find rides, follow a ride and check you in and out. It also uses your location in the background while you're online as a driver or on a ride. If you turn this off in your phone's settings, you can't ride or drive.
  • A phone's position can be wrong. If a check-in, check-out or payment went wrong because of it, you can appeal.
  • Becoming a verified driver is your choice. It uses your camera and your phone's NFC reader to read your ID, as the privacy policy explains.
  • Background location uses more battery. Your mobile data costs are your own.
  • The app keeps a random ID for your phone, so that a strike or ban stays on it.
