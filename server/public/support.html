How can we help?

In danger? Call the emergency number first.That's 112 in Europe. Then tell us what happened.

Rides

How do I book a ride?

Tap the map or "Where to?", pick a driver and tap Book. A driver has 5 minutes to accept, then you have 5 minutes to pay from your own wallet. You can also tap "Your price" to name what you want to pay, and let a driver nearby claim it.

When do I check in?

Once you're inside the vehicle, after checking the number plate. Being at the pickup isn't enough. The check-in screen opens by itself when your driver is within 100 m.

My driver is late

Once your driver is past their time to reach the pickup, a green button lets you cancel for a full refund, until you've checked in. Look at the map first: if they're clearly on the way, you may prefer to wait.

Something went wrong with a ride

Open the ride under Rides and tap "Something went wrong" within 24 hours. The money waits while we look at it.

What do I get back if I cancel?

Before you've paid it's free. Once you've paid you get 50% back, or everything if your driver is late or cancels. A planned ride is free to cancel until 3 hours before. All of it is in the terms.

Paying

How do I pay?

With USDC or SOL on Solana, from your own wallet app, or in cash. In a coin: once a driver has accepted, tap Pay with your wallet, approve it there and come back. The money waits in escrow until the ride is done.

How does paying in cash work?

Pick Cash when you order and pay your driver the whole price in the car. You don't need a wallet, and you check in and out as on any ride. Once your driver has checked out, the ride says Paid in cash. Drivezzz never handles the cash, so we can't refund it. Drivers who don't take cash can turn it off in Wallet.

Where is my crypto after I cancelled?

If you hadn't paid yet, nothing moved. If you had, it goes back to the wallet you paid with, as the terms say: everything within about a minute, or half after 24 hours, the same time your driver gets the other half.

Does paying cost anything extra?

The network fee, about €0.001, and a small deposit of about €0.10 to €0.25 for the escrow, which comes back by itself when it closes. Drivezzz's own fee of €0.10 per ride paid in crypto comes out of the driver's payout, so it isn't added to your price.

Can Drivezzz get my wallet back?

No. Your wallet and its recovery words are yours, and we never see them. Never type your recovery words into a website or send them to anyone, us included.

Driving

What do I need to drive?

A vehicle with photos of its front and back, and for a car also the inside, and a connected wallet for the coin you want to be paid in. To drive a car in the Netherlands you also need to be verified, which gets you a blue check. Drivezzz is a marketplace and doesn't take responsibility for your insurance or licences: we check what we can, and having what the law asks stays up to you.

How do I change my wallet?

Your first wallet connects straight away. After that it's locked, so nobody who gets into your account can send your money elsewhere. In Wallet, tap Request wallet change and tell us why. Once we say yes you can change it once, and then it locks again. Rides already paid still go to the wallet they were paid with.

When do drivers get paid?

24 hours after check-out, to the wallet they connected, unless there's an appeal. On a ride paid in crypto and paid out in full, €0.10 of it goes to Drivezzz as its fee.

Why was I put offline?

You didn't answer a ride within 5 minutes. Go online again when you're ready.

The rider doesn't show up

Stop at the pickup: your 10 minutes of waiting start when the car stands still. After that you can cancel with "Rider not there" to claim 50%.

Safety and reports

I feel unsafe during a ride

Call the emergency number first (112 in Europe). If your phone and the car drift apart, the app asks if everything is OK.

How do I report or block someone?

Open their page, the ride or the chat and tap ··· to report or block them. Blocking means you're no longer matched with each other. We look at reports within 24 hours.

A rider entered a drop-off a car can't reach

Always report this. Open the drive and tap "Something went wrong", then tick "Impossible drop-off location", or tap ··· and report the rider. The drive is added to your report, so we can see the planned route on a map. It can get the rider banned permanently, straight away.

I want to report illegal content, without an account

Use the form below with the topic "Report illegal content". Say what it is, where you saw it and why you think it's illegal. We look at it within 24 hours and answer by email.

I got a strike or a ban

Every strike is a one-week ban, and three strikes is a permanent ban. It stays on your phone, also after reinstalling. You can appeal on the ban screen, once every 24 hours.

Your account and data

I got a new phone

Add an email and password in Profile, then log in with them on the new phone.

Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes. Use the form below, and we answer within a month. The privacy policy says what we keep.

Is Drivezzz open source?

Its code is public on GitHub: the iPhone and Android apps, the server, this website and the escrow program. You may read it, check it and build your own on it, with your own smart contract, under the Drivezzz Source Licence. Copying Drivezzz itself isn't allowed, so strictly it's source-available rather than open source.

Delete your account

In the app: Profile, then Delete account. Finish or cancel your rides first, also planned ones. It happens at once.

Without the app, or if it no longer works for you: send us a message saying you want your account deleted, with your user ID (it starts with dz-, at the top of Profile) or the email of your login. We check it's your account and delete it within 30 days, the same way.

Your name, email, login, messages, vehicles' plates and photos, and connected wallets go. Rides and payments stay without your name, as far as needed for accounts and disputes, and a ban stays on its phone. The privacy policy has all of it.

While you're banned it can't be deleted: we keep the record of the phone. You can still ask us for your data, or appeal.
