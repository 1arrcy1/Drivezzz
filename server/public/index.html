How it works

From a tap to the drop-off.

Escrow

Your money waits, on the blockchain.

From your wallet, not ours.Drivezzz has no deposits, balances or withdrawals, and never sees your wallet's keys.
One ride, one escrow.Each payment waits in its own escrow, a program on Solana, until the ride is done. Then it goes to your driver, or back to you if you're owed a refund.
It comes back by itself.If nothing is decided within 30 days, the whole payment can be sent back to the wallet that paid. Anyone can start that, even if Drivezzz no longer exists.
€0.10 goes to Drivezzz.That's the platform's fee on a ride paid in crypto. It comes out of the driver's payout, in the ride's coin, when a ride is paid out in full. Cash rides, refunds and a 50/50 have no fee.
Check the code yourself.The apps, the server and the escrow program are public on GitHub. See what they do, or build your own on them.

If plans change

You know what you get back.

You cancel before you've paidFree. No money has moved.
Your driver is lateEverything back, until you've checked in
Your driver cancelsEverything back, also once you're in the car, unless they waited 10 minutes at the pickup for you
You cancel after paying, with your driver on time50% back, 50% to your driver

Planned rides and the rest are in the terms. Crypto can lose value, and a payment on a blockchain can't be reversed.

Drive

Your car, your hours, your price.

  • Set your own price per km, per minute and for the way to your rider, for each vehicle.
  • Drive a car, a motor or a scooter.
  • See the route, the distance and the price before you accept.
  • Plan drives ahead if you want to, and go online only when it suits you.
  • Get paid to your own wallet 24 hours after a ride.
  • Pay Drivezzz €0.10 per ride paid in crypto, out of your payout, and nothing on cash rides.
You drive for yourself

Drivezzz is at its core a marketplace and doesn't take responsibility for your insurance or licences. We do check what we can against the sources that exist: verify to get a blue check, which a car in the Netherlands needs before it can go online. Having what the law asks stays up to you.

Safety

Built for two strangers in one car.

Check the plate

See the car, its photos and its number plate. You check in only once you're inside.

Both phones follow the route

If your phone and the car drift apart, the app asks if everything is OK.

Private messages

Messages are end-to-end encrypted. We can only read a chat if you report it.

Report or block anyone

From their page, a ride or a chat. We look at reports within 24 hours. Always report a drop-off a car can't reach: it can get someone banned permanently, straight away.

Three strikes and out

A strike is a week's ban, three is for good. A new account or a reinstall doesn't get around it.

On-time and completed scores

Every driver's page shows how often they're on time and finish a ride, with real reviews from their riders.
