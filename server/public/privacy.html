Privacy

Last updated 10 November 2026. What we keep about you, why, for how long, who else sees it, and how to delete it.

In short

  • We keep what's needed to run your rides. We don't sell your data, show ads, track you across other apps or use analytics tools.
  • Your location is the most sensitive part: we use it to find rides, to follow a ride, and to check you in and out. Positions kept with a ride are deleted after 90 days.
  • Your ride payments are public on the Solana blockchain and can't be removed from it.
  • You can delete your account in the app, in Profile.

How we get it

From you, when you fill something in, take a photo or connect a wallet. From your phone, such as your location, once you've allowed it. From the Solana blockchain, where we see your payment arrive. And from the Dutch vehicle register (RDW), for the details that go with a number plate.

Who sees what

  • Riders see online drivers' positions, reviews, vehicles, plates and rates.
  • Drivers see an order's rough pickup area and destination. The exact pickup comes when they take the ride.
  • For a ride they share, a rider sees their driver's name and position. A driver sees the rider's name, the pickup spot and the drop-off, but never where the rider is.
  • Routes you save stay on your own phone. We don't get them.
  • Messages between you and a driver are end-to-end encrypted: we only store them scrambled and can't read them. Their word filter runs on your own phone, before a message is encrypted, so we don't see what it checks. If you report someone you've messaged, your phone adds your chat with them to the report, and then we can read that chat.
  • Our admins see what's needed to handle reports and appeals, including both routes of a ride.

Outside the EU

Apple, Google and Cloudflare, and the services connecting a wallet, can process data outside the European Economic Area, mainly in the United States. That happens under the EU's approved safeguards: the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. OpenFreeMap, Photon and the OSRM route server are run by organisations in the EU, though OpenFreeMap may use Cloudflare too. The Solana blockchain has no location: it's copied around the world.

The blockchain

A payment on Solana is public and permanent. Anyone can see the wallet that paid, the driver's wallet, the amounts and the time, and nobody can remove it, us included. We don't publish your name with it, but someone who knows your wallet address can link it to you. If you'd rather not have that link, use a wallet you don't use for anything else.

Automatic decisions

Some things happen by themselves: who gets offered a ride, a driver going offline after not answering, the on-time and completed scores, and payouts and refunds that fall due. These follow from the terms. A person decides appeals, strikes and bans, and you can always ask for a person to look at something that affects you.

Your permissions

You choose what the app may use, and you can change your mind at any time in your phone's Settings: on an iPhone under Drivezzz, on Android under Apps, then Drivezzz. That doesn't affect what happened before.

  • Location. "While using the app" is enough; we never ask for "Always". While a driver is online or on a ride, the blue status bar on an iPhone or a notification on Android shows that it's in use. On Android that notification also shows while an order waits for a driver. Going offline ends it. On an iPhone closing the app ends it too; on Android a driver stays online with the app closed, until they go offline in the app or in that notification.
  • Notifications. Turning them off means you may miss a driver accepting or an offer.
  • Photos. You pick a vehicle's photos in your phone's own picker. The app only gets the ones you pick, and asks for no access to your library or camera.

Deleting your account

Open Profile in the app and tap Delete account, once your rides are finished or cancelled. It happens at once.

  • What goes: your name, email and login, your messages, position, notification token, connected wallets and requests to change them, your vehicles' plates and photos, your bio, who you blocked, and the text of reviews you wrote or received.
  • What stays, without your name: your rides and payments, as far as needed for accounts and disputes, with star ratings and when you accepted the End User Licence Agreement. Reports and appeals, as far as needed for safety.
  • What stays on the phone's record: the phone's random ID, the accounts used on it, and any strikes and bans.

While you're banned you can't delete your account: we keep it, and the phone's record, to keep the ban working. You can still appeal and use your other rights. No app? Ask through the support page.

Your rights

You can see, correct, take a copy of or delete your data, limit how we use it, and object to what we do on the basis of our legitimate interest. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it. Most of it is in the app; for the rest use the support page, and we answer within a month.

You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or the one in your own country.

Security

Traffic to our server is encrypted. Passwords are only stored as a hash, messages are end-to-end encrypted, and the app never sees your wallet's keys. No system is perfect: if something goes wrong that puts you at risk, we tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

This website

This website has no analytics, ads or trackers. If you pick a colour, it's remembered in your own browser. Cloudflare may set a cookie that's needed to keep the site safe.

Our short links, such as drivezzz.s3rver.nl/shortlink, send you on to the App Store or Google Play and count how often they're used: per day, whether it was an iPhone, an Android phone or something else, and the country. Nothing in that count points to you, and it sets no cookie.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters to you, we tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
